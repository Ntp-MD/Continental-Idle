# Research file 0U — Uncertainty, assumption status and provenance (how to label what you know)

Scope: the labelling discipline an architecture agent needs on a host grid where most architectural truth
is *not expressible*. Defines the status taxonomy (the spec's 8 classes + "not representable"), the
mandatory assumption register, confidence calibration without fake precision, and provenance rules.
Formalises what `building-codes.md` already practises in *How to flag instead of fake* — it extends that
flag block rather than inventing a parallel one.

Host grid: `1 tile = 0.5 m`, per-floor 2D, tile states exactly `walkable | blocked | door` (verified:
`src/blueprint-editor/domain/schema/walkable.ts:4`), rooms by flood-fill + fixture tags, A\* octile
movement, lift portals + queues, tag access control; no heights, materials, structural objects, climate
model, time or cost. Repo-verified ceilings: `MAX_GRID_ROWS = MAX_GRID_COLUMNS = 256`, `MAX_FLOORS = 100`,
`MAX_TAGS = 256`, `MIN_STREET_WIDTH_TILES = 5` (`src/blueprint-editor/limits.ts`). Repo-verified negative:
no physical-unit constant exists in `src/` (grep `metres|metersPer|METERS|mPerTile` → nothing), so **the
metre-per-tile scale is a convention, not an engine fact** — register row A1.

## Rules

### UN-01 Tag every load-bearing claim with status + source + confidence at first mention
- Rule: Any quantity, requirement or causal claim a later decision rests on must carry an inline status tag the moment it is asserted: `[U:<class>|s:<source token or UNCITED>|c:<High|Medium|Low>]`. No tag = the reader is entitled to treat it as uncited prose; an untagged design is an invalid deliverable.
- Evidence: Analysts must "Properly distinguish between underlying intelligence information and analysts' assumptions and judgements", "Properly describe the quality and credibility of underlying sources, data and methodologies", and "Properly express and explain uncertainties associated with major analytic judgments" (ICD 203 tradecraft standards). Provenance theory supplies the structure to attach: every value is an *Entity* generated by an *Activity* and attributable to an *Agent* (W3C PROV-O).
- Source: ODNI *Objectivity* tradecraft page https://archive.dni.gov/index.php/how-we-work/objectivity and ICD 203 https://archive.dni.gov/files/documents/ICD/ICD-203.pdf — T1 (directive PDF located, body not machine-readable; quotes from the HTML page); W3C, *PROV-O*, Recommendation 30 Apr 2013 (Lebo, Sahoo, McGuinness) https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/ — T1.
- Class: CODE REQUIREMENT (of this skill, not of a building code)
- Scope: universal; every rule below is a special case of UN-01
- Confidence: High
- Grid translation: Tags live in the report, not the tile data (the schema has no field for them — UN-05). Canonical set: `[U:known|s:IBC2021§1018.2|c:High]`, `[U:assumed|s:brief-gap|c:Medium|owner:<role>]`, `[U:est|s:T3 rule-of-thumb|band:±30%|c:Medium]`, `[U:unknown|q:<question>|owner:<role>]`, `[U:research]`, `[U:eng-verify]`, `[U:code-verify]`, `[U:prof-review]`, `[U:NR|needs:<missing field>]`. Validation: reject any deliverable quoting a numeric dimension with zero tags.
- Exceptions / failure mode: Arithmetic from the grid definition needs `s:derived`, not a citation. Failure symptom: a plausible spec sheet where code minima and inventions are indistinguishable — the documented harm of models that "tend to be overconfident" while users "rely heavily on LM generations" (Zhou et al., ACL 2024).

### UN-02 Split what more information would fix from what no information can fix
- Rule: Classify every open item as *reducible* (missing data or model — go and get it, or bound it) or *irreducible* (real variability: future demand, occupant behaviour, weather). Never promise to "verify away" an irreducible quantity; publish a band and a decision rule instead.
- Evidence: Epistemic uncertainty carries the "possibility to reduce them by gathering more data"; aleatory is one the "modeler does not foresee the possibility of reducing them" (Der Kiureghian & Ditlevsen, *Structural Safety* 31, 2009). ML restatement: aleatoric is "noise inherent in observations", epistemic is "uncertainty in the model... explained away given enough data" (Kendall & Gal, NIPS 2017).
- Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0167473008000556 (abstract via https://orbit.dtu.dk/en/publications/aleatoric-or-epistemic-does-it-matter/) — T2; https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.04977 — T2.
- Class: FACT (methodological taxonomy)
- Scope: universal
- Confidence: High (split), Medium (that the boundary is always crisp — see *Weak or contested*)
- Grid translation: Two registers, never one. `UNKNOWN (reducible)` gets `q:`/`owner:` + a research step; `ESTIMATED` gets `band:` and stays an estimate. Validation: an item tagged `[U:unknown]` whose answer is future behaviour ("will guests cross the lobby?") is misfiled — re-tag `[U:est|band:…]` with scenarios.
- Exceptions / failure mode: Occupancy, arrival patterns and routing policy are irreducible yet *are* the driver for egress and lift sizing, so the answer is a scenario set, never a verification task. Failure symptom: "verify actual occupancy later" while designing to one best guess.

### UN-03 No digit that the weakest input cannot support
- Rule: A derived number may not carry more significant figures than its least precise input, and must be rounded to its band. This bans "12.7 m²" from a rule of thumb and "3.4 persons" as a capacity.
- Evidence: Uncertainty reporting requires giving "some indication of the values of the components of uncertainty not taken into account" (NIST TN 1297 App. D6, GUM-based); estimate-accuracy practice publishes bands rather than points, from Class 5 (−50/+100 % at 0–2 % definition) to Class 1 (−10/+15 % at 65–100 %) (AACE RP 18R-97).
- Source: https://www.nist.gov/pml/nist-technical-note-1297/nist-tn-1297-appendix-d6-uncertainty-and-units-si-proper-use-si-and — T1; AACE 18R-97 TOC https://web.aacei.org/docs/default-source/toc/toc_18r-97.pdf (paywalled body) with bands from https://cenex.au/aace/estimate-classification.html — T1/T4.
- Class: STANDARD
- Scope: universal
- Confidence: High
- Grid translation: Compute the quantum first: 1 tile = 0.5 m linear, 0.25 m² in area, so a 12×10-tile room (30.0 m² shell) moves ±(W+H)·0.25 m² = ±5.5 m² for a one-tile change in either direction — a second decimal is meaningless. Emit areas as `X tiles (≈Y m², ±1 tile)`, capacities as integers with a scenario label. Validation: reject any stated precision finer than 0.5 m / 0.25 m².
- Exceptions / failure mode: Grid integers (tile counts, room counts, portal counts) are genuinely precise, as is octile cost as a *model* quantity. Failure symptom: "travel distance 23.618 m" implying survey accuracy the tile model cannot deliver.

### UN-04 Publish the accuracy band and the stage with every estimate
- Rule: Every estimate issues as `value ± band (stage)`, where the stage names the definition level that produced it. No estimate without a band; no band without a named stage.
- Evidence: AACE's classification ties achievable accuracy to how much of the design is defined, and presents the ranges as "typical values observed across completed projects and serve as guidelines, not targets" — the band is empirical, not aspirational.
- Source: https://cenex.au/aace/estimate-classification.html — T4; AACE RP 18R-97 TOC — T1 (title located, body paywalled).
- Class: STANDARD
- Scope: universal; percentages are discipline-specific
- Confidence: High (principle), Medium (the specific percentages, T4 mirror)
- Grid translation: Three stages with default bands: S1 concept (module counts only) ±40 %; S2 schematic (room envelopes placed) ±20 %; S3 set-out (all tiles assigned, tags complete) ±5 %. Report area sums, circulation share, capacity and adjacency satisfaction at the stage that produced them. Validation: an estimate quoted tighter than its stage band is a violation; force the stage band.
- Exceptions / failure mode: Pure grid arithmetic is exact at every stage — do not pad it with a fake ±. Failure symptom: "the floor is 21.4 % circulation" at S1, where one party wall moves the number 3 points.

### UN-05 Unknown and Not-representable are different failures; say which
- Rule: When the model has no field that can carry a property (fire rating, ceiling height, material, sound insulation, span, time, cost), the item is `Not representable (NR)`: not `Unknown`, not researchable, and never encoded as a plausible tile value. For each NR item record exactly one response: proxy (with its fidelity claim), external escalation (UN-16), or abstention (UN-20).
- Evidence: Validation asks "if the model accurately represents the real world application" — a property of the model, not the data (ASME V&V). Deliverable practice encodes the same limit: the LOD specification "does not prescribe what Levels of Development are to be reached at what point" and exists to fix what each element may be used for; AIA G201 exists to set the "permitted uses" of digital data. Decision science adds the third category beyond risk and uncertainty — ignorance, which the frame itself cannot carry (Oehmen & Kwakkel 2022).
- Source: https://www.asme.org/codes-standards/publications-information/verification-validation-uncertainty — T1/T3; BIM Forum *LOD Specification* 2025 https://bimforum.org/resource/lod-level-of-development-lod-specification/ — T3; AIA G201/G202 FAQs https://help.aiacontracts.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500010380681-faqs-g201-2013-project-digital-data-protocol-form-and-g202-2013-project-building-information-modeling-form — T3; Oehmen & Kwakkel https://research.tudelft.nl/en/publications/risk-uncertainty-and-ignorance-in-engineering-systems-design/ — T2.
- Class: DESIGN PRINCIPLE
- Scope: universal on this host
- Confidence: High — tile-state enum and limits read from the repo this session; those limits are the facts, while the NR-versus-Unknown response discipline is the design principle
- Grid translation: Keep a *capability ledger*: `representable | proxyable | NR`. Wall presence → representable (1 blocked tile); wall thickness → model constant (0.5 m), therefore never a design output; fire rating, material, height, acoustic performance, travel time → NR; door clear width → proxyable at 2-tile (1.0 m) granularity; lift quality-of-service → NR (no time model). Never write tile geometry that *looks* like the missing property (e.g. thickening a wall to "show" fire separation).
- Exceptions / failure mode: Tags can hold arbitrary strings, so `fire_rating:2h` can be *typed* — that is a `proxy (no consumer)`, and any claim about it still goes to UN-16. Failure symptom: the agent believes the model holds the property because a tag has text, and reports compliance.

### UN-06 Match the response to the kind of open question
- Rule: Sort every open item into one of four kinds and apply the matching response, never a generic "flag it": **risk** (objective known, outcome unknown → treat/mitigate), **uncertainty** (range probable → bound it, design robustly), **deep uncertainty** (models, futures and values all disputed → seek options that perform across scenarios instead of optimising one), **ignorance** (the frame cannot express it → escalate or abstain).
- Evidence: "Risk, a corollary of uncertainty, is understood as the effect of uncertainty on objectives", with classic risk management for known risks, "robust decision-making for uncertainty" and the precautionary principle for ignorance; and "you cannot not manage risk — even ignoring risk equates to a decision to accept it" (Oehmen & Kwakkel 2022). Deep uncertainty covers decisions where "models are uncertain, outcomes are unknown", spanning system behaviour, future states and "what is acceptable"; the response is strategies that "perform well across many futures" rather than "reliance on single best estimates" (Lempert et al. 2024).
- Source: as UN-05 — T2; https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/climate/articles/10.3389/fclim.2024.1380054/full — T2.
- Class: DESIGN PRINCIPLE
- Scope: universal
- Confidence: Medium-High (categories are defined; assigning an item to one is judgement)
- Grid translation: Prefix open items: `RISK:` (plan may fail an egress minimum once local adoption is known), `UNC:` (occupant-load band), `DEEP:` (whether simulated guests path-optimise at all; which metric governs), `IG:`/`NR:` (material, height). Validation: any `DEEP:` item resolved by a *single* numeric assumption with no second scenario is a UN-06 failure.
- Exceptions / failure mode: There is no economy model here, so cost risk is not a live category — do not manufacture it. Failure symptom: answering a `DEEP:` question with one A\* number and presenting it as a finding.

### UN-07 Maintain the five-field assumption register on every design; it is mandatory output
- Rule: Wherever requirements are incomplete, produce a register with the spec's five mandatory fields per row — Assumption / Reason / Confidence / Impact if wrong / Verification needed — plus UN-08's three governance fields. It ships with the design, machine-readable, never folded into prose.
- Evidence: Assumptions in management practice are "uncertainties that could affect the project", recorded as "to be confirmed" items; if false they "become risks or issues requiring immediate action" (PRINCE2 RAID). The duty to separate assumptions from information is T1 (ICD 203). Standardised structured documents are the accepted remedy for unstated model characteristics (datasheets, model cards).
- Source: https://www.projex.com/how-to-use-raid-properly-in-prince2-7th-edition/ — T4; https://archive.dni.gov/index.php/how-we-work/objectivity — T1; Gebru et al., *Datasheets for Datasets* https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.09010 (CACM 2021) — T2; Garbin & Marques https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8980932/ — T2.
- Class: CODE REQUIREMENT (of this skill)
- Scope: universal
- Confidence: High
- Grid translation: Schema and a worked hotel register are in `## Assumption register schema`. Validation: `every [U:assumed] tag in the report has a register row`, and `every register row backs at least one report claim` (otherwise delete it).
- Exceptions / failure mode: A brief with no gaps still gets a register stating "no material assumptions; the following are facts: …" so silence is not mistaken for completeness. Failure symptom: six unstated readings of the brief inherited by the reviewer.

### UN-08 Give every assumption an owner, a revisit trigger and a reversibility grade
- Rule: Extend each row with `Owner` (who can settle it), `Revisit trigger` (event that forces re-checking), `Reversibility` (rework if it proves false). An assumption with no owner is not an assumption — it is a decision the agent took silently.
- Evidence: A failed assumption must surface as a tracked risk or issue, which presupposes ownership and a review cadence (PRINCE2 RAID, T4); "monitor and review" sits inside the risk process (ISO 31000:2018, cited by title/edition). Reversibility as a decision grade is standard: type-1 decisions are "one-way doors – and these decisions must be made methodically, carefully, slowly", type-2 are "changeable, reversible... made quickly by high judgment individuals" (Amazon 2016 letter, SEC filing).
- Source: https://www.projex.com/how-to-use-raid-properly-in-prince2-7th-edition/ — T4; ISO 31000:2018 *Risk management — Guidelines* (catalogue https://www.iso.org/standard/65694.html returned 403; title/edition only) — T1; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000119312516530910/d168744dex991.htm — T3.
- Class: STANDARD
- Scope: universal
- Confidence: Medium-High (PRINCE2/ISO texts partly unread; the reversibility source is a corporate letter, not a standard)
- Grid translation: Owners restricted to real roles: `brief-owner`, `engine`, `agent`, `code-consultant`, `structural`, `mep`, `fire`. Triggers as engine events: "first save exceeding 200×200 tiles", "MAX_TAGS reached", "stair required above floor 2", "sim gains a time model". Reversibility by rework volume: `tile-local` (<50 tiles), `floor` (room regrouping), `section` (core relocation), `concept` (module change).
- Exceptions / failure mode: An assumption owned by nobody but the agent should be *decided* and logged, not carried — the operating brief says so. Failure symptom: 20 rows all marked "TBD", none actionable.

### UN-09 Settle high-impact × low-confidence assumptions before committing geometry; else take the reversible branch
- Rule: Rank open assumptions by impact-if-wrong against inverse confidence. High-impact/low-confidence items must be resolved, or made moot (geometry that does not depend on them), before walls are final. If unsettled, choose the cheapest-to-reverse branch and record that you did so.
- Evidence: Deep-uncertainty method prescribes options that "perform well across many futures" instead of optimising one forecast (Lempert et al. 2024); irreversible decisions warrant slow, deliberate treatment while reversible ones are delegated and fast (Amazon 2016, T3); a false assumption converts into an incident needing action (PRINCE2 RAID, T4).
- Source: as UN-06 / UN-08 — T2 / T3 / T4.
- Class: DESIGN PRINCIPLE
- Scope: universal
- Confidence: High (principle), Low (any specific scoring formula — do not invent weights)
- Grid translation: Commit points in dependency order: (1) grid scale + storey count, (2) core positions (lift portals), (3) party-wall / separation geometry, (4) door positions, (5) fixture tags, (6) finishes (absent). Items touching 1–3 are `concept`-irreversible → settle or make moot; items touching 4–6 may ship flagged. Validation: any still-open `concept`-grade assumption at finalisation emits a BLOCKER.
- Exceptions / failure mode: This project's standing order (floors may be wiped at any time) lowers rework cost, so it relaxes the *ask-gate*, never the *labelling* duty: assume, log, continue. Failure symptom: stopping to ask a question the brief already authorised, or designing the tower to an unstated floor-to-floor value.

### UN-10 State confidence as a named rung plus its evidence basis, never a bare percentage
- Rule: Confidence is `High | Medium | Low` **plus the reason** — the type, amount, quality and consistency of the evidence and the degree of agreement. Percentages are allowed only when the cited source itself states them. "90% sure" from an unquantified judgement is banned.
- Evidence: IPCC: confidence depends on "the type, amount, quality, and consistency of evidence and the degree of agreement", with terms "very low, low, medium, high, and very high", the evidence base being "mechanistic understanding, theory, data, models, expert judgment". GRADE operationalises the same with four certainty ratings and named downgrade domains (risk of bias, inconsistency, indirectness, imprecision, publication bias). Estimative words were invented to stop vague language posing as knowledge (Kent).
- Source: IPCC AR5 *Uncertainties Guidance Note* https://www.ipcc.ch/site/assets/uploads/2017-08/AR5_Uncertainty_Guidance_Note.pdf (PDF not machine-readable) with wording verified via https://www.greenfacts.org/en/climate-change-ar5-science-basis/l-3/1-likelihood.htm — T1 primary / T4 mirror; CDC ACIP GRADE handbook ch.7 https://www.cdc.gov/acip-grade-handbook/hcp/chapter-7-grade-criteria-determining-certainty-of-evidence/index.html — T1; Kent, *Words of Estimative Probability* https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP93T01132R000100020036-3.pdf with scale values from https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Words_of_estimative_probability — T1 primary / T4 mirror.
- Class: STANDARD
- Scope: universal
- Confidence: High (structure), Medium (the numeric WEP equivalents, mirror-sourced)
- Grid translation: Fixed rungs: **High** = T1 primary opened, or arithmetic from the grid spec, or ≥2 independent T2/T3 agreeing; **Medium** = a single T3, or a T1 whose body could not be opened, or a T4 mirror of a paywalled primary; **Low** = T4/T5 only, recollected rule, or the value is a proxy in a model that cannot hold it. Render as `c:High(basis: T1 opened, 2 sources agree)` — the basis is mandatory.
- Exceptions / failure mode: Do not average rungs (Medium ≠ 0.6) and do not treat repeated citation of the same secondary lineage as independent agreement. Failure symptom: one vendor blog quoted three times, promoting a Low claim to "well documented".

### UN-11 Aggregate confidence along a derivation chain by the weakest link, and re-aggregate on change
- Rule: B computed from A can never exceed A's confidence and inherits A's downgrade reasons. Three chained derived numbers are one number wearing three hats, not three agreeing sources. When any input's status changes, re-tag every dependent line.
- Evidence: GRADE's mechanics are subtractive: certainty falls for risk of bias, inconsistency, indirectness, imprecision and publication bias, and rises only for specific reasons (strength of association, dose-response, plausible confounding) (CDC ACIP). AACE bands have the same property — definition level, not arithmetic, caps achievable accuracy. NIST requires disclosing the components left out (UN-03).
- Source: as UN-10, UN-03, UN-04 — T1.
- Class: STANDARD
- Scope: universal
- Confidence: High
- Grid translation: Print the chain in the line: `travel 24 m [U:est|c:Medium|chain: factor T1(opened) × room count derived × scale ASSUMED(A1)]`. Because the scale is an assumption, every metre-denominated output inherits Medium at best — declare that once at the top, then reference it. Validation: reject any `c:High` line whose `chain:` contains a Medium or Low term.
- Exceptions / failure mode: Topology facts (adjacency, flood-fill membership, reachable/unreachable under a tag rule) may be High as *exact-in-model*, never as *true-of-the-building*. Failure symptom: "the plan is compliant" derived from numbers whose weakest link was a guess.

### UN-12 Every citation carries its tier; code citations also carry jurisdiction + edition + anchor
- Rule: Tier each source on the 5-level scale and keep the tier attached to the claim. For regulatory sources, jurisdiction + edition + clause anchor are part of the citation; a bare "the IBC says" is unverifiable and counts as uncited.
- Evidence: This is the operating rule already in `building-codes.md` ("Name the edition and jurisdiction, or say you can't… 'IBC 2021 §1018.2 (model, verify local adoption)' is auditable"), and it matches the spec's source hierarchy. PROV-O gives the structural form: values trace through derivations (`wasDerivedFrom`, `hadPrimarySource`, `wasRevisionOf`) to a primary source.
- Source: `architecture-skill/research/building-codes.md`, *How to flag instead of fake* (in-repo, canonical); https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/ — T1.
- Class: CODE REQUIREMENT (of this skill)
- Scope: universal
- Confidence: High
- Grid translation: Tokens: T1 official/standards/government, T2 peer-reviewed/university, T3 established professional publication or textbook, T4 professional community, T5 anecdote. Render `T<n>` plus, for codes, `<jurisdiction>:<edition>:<clause>`. Validation: a numeric dimension adjacent to a T4/T5 token is an automatic UN-13 violation.
- Exceptions / failure mode: A source whose record was verified but whose body could not be opened is cited at its tier with `text not read this pass` — the convention used across the sibling files. Failure symptom: confident paraphrase of a standard nobody retrieved.

### UN-13 Never launder a low tier into a rule
- Rule: T4/T5 may justify a *method*, a *vocabulary* or a *question*, never alone a dimension, a threshold or a compliance statement. A number whose only provenance is a vendor blog becomes an estimate with a band and a verification route, or disappears.
- Evidence: Fabricated citations are measurable: across 56,381 papers (2.2 M citations) 1.07 % of papers contain invalid citations, rising in the AI-assisted era (GhostCite); a benchmark exists because "we have no standardized way to measure how well tools detect citation hallucinations", and 53 NeurIPS 2025 papers were flagged for fabricated citations (HALLMARK).
- Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2602.06718v2 — T2; https://github.com/rpatrik96/hallmark — T2/T3.
- Class: STANDARD
- Scope: universal
- Confidence: High (prohibition), Medium (prevalence figures, single recent study)
- Grid translation: Tier policy: numbers → T1/T2 or opened T3 primary; compliance claims → T1 with jurisdiction+edition; method/vocabulary → any tier, labelled; heuristic bands → T3/T4 with `c:Low` + `band:`. Validation: print the tier beside every numeric claim and rewrite failures as `[U:est|s:T4-only|band:±30%|c:Low]` or delete.
- Exceptions / failure mode: Where the only evidence for a needed dimension is T4 (the situation for most non-residential room minima here), the correct output is "no number — here is the computation from fixtures + capacity + flow", not a laundered figure. Failure symptom: "hotels typically have 4.2 m² bathrooms" with no citable basis.

### UN-14 Cite only what you opened; an unknown section number stays unknown
- Rule: A citation must name an artefact actually retrieved this session. If the rule is remembered but the anchor is unverified, write `section: UNKNOWN (verify)` at confidence Low. Never reconstruct a clause number, page, DOI or edition from memory.
- Evidence: Failure is documented at scale (GhostCite, HALLMARK). Model self-assessment is uneven: models "can evaluate the validity of their own claims" and are "well-calibrated on diverse multiple choice" but degrade on knowing-whether-they-know for new tasks (Kadavath et al. 2022), and verbalised uncertainty still misses the human gradations it imitates (arXiv:2507.10587).
- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.05221 — T2; https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.10587 — T2; https://arxiv.org/html/2602.06718v2 — T2.
- Class: STANDARD
- Scope: universal
- Confidence: High
- Grid translation: The house flag block's `ATTEMPTED SOURCE:` field is where the honest negative goes — doc, edition, jurisdiction tried and the failure mode (403 / paywall / binary PDF). Validation: any URL not fetched this session is re-tagged `UNCITED — verify`, and the claim it supported drops to Low.
- Exceptions / failure mode: Books may be cited by title + edition with `(no stable URL retrieved; cited by edition)`, at method level only. Failure symptom: a citation that resolves to a different paper than the claim it supports.

### UN-15 Never claim compliance; claim consistency with a clause as read, plus a flag
- Rule: The agent does not certify. Permitted form: "consistent with `<clause>` as read in `<edition>` (`<jurisdiction>` adoption UNVERIFIED)", always with the structured flag naming what would close it. Compliance determination belongs to registered, competency-assessed parties.
- Evidence: Building work needs approval from a building control body — "You may need approval from a building control body"; only limited categories self-certify ("An installer… who's registered with a scheme can self-certify that their work complies"), with a certificate issued within 8 weeks (GOV.UK). Those who make such determinations "must be registered as a building inspector" and must first have "passed an independent competency assessment" (Building Safety Regulator). The professional's own duty is bounded: "reasonable skill and care" by a "competent professional", and it is "not a guarantee of perfect results".
- Source: https://www.gov.uk/building-regulations-approval — T1; https://buildingsafety.campaign.gov.uk/building-safety-regulator-making-buildings-safer/building-safety-regulator-news/regulating-the-building-control-profession/ — T1; https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Standard_of_care — T4 (legal principle at wiki level; primary case law not retrieved).
- Class: CODE REQUIREMENT
- Scope: universal where a regulatory regime exists
- Confidence: High; the reserved certification duty (registered, competency-assessed building-control parties) is the code requirement, while the permitted wording form is our house standard
- Grid translation: Banned strings: "code-compliant", "meets IBC", "approved", "ADA-compliant". Replacement pattern: `consistent with <clause> (<edition>, <jurisdiction> adoption UNVERIFIED) — flag: <what would confirm>`, using the 7-field block from `building-codes.md` verbatim. Geometry that cannot express the regulated quantity (rating, enclosure, mm clearances) is escalated as NR + flag, never asserted.
- Exceptions / failure mode: Where a proxy exists (2-tile door = 1.0 m vs a 32 in / 0.813 m minimum), stating the *relation and its margin* is permitted — "0.19 m of slack at the assumed scale, so a scale change above ~1.2× breaks it". Failure symptom: "egress is compliant" because three corridor tiles were used.

### UN-16 Route each unresolved item to the right external authority class
- Rule: Choose deliberately among `Requires research`, `Requires engineering verification`, `Requires code verification`, `Requires professional review` — they differ in who can close them and with what evidence. Never leave a generic "TBD", and never send a researchable question to a licensed professional: that devalues the escalation.
- Evidence: Competence boundaries are institutional facts: only registered, competency-assessed inspectors may perform building-control functions (BSR page, UN-15); the professional's duty runs to care within competence, not omniscience (standard-of-care sources, UN-15); and whether a model is adequate for a use is a validation question owned by the model's owner, not its user (ASME V&V, UN-05).
- Source: as UN-15 — T1/T4; as UN-05 — T1/T3.
- Class: STANDARD
- Scope: universal
- Confidence: High
- Grid translation: Routing table for the `Verification needed` field: `research` = a document holds the answer (clause, catalogue, dataset) → agent does it now; `eng-verify` = analysis whose inputs the model lacks (loads, spans, services) → engineer; `code-verify` = clause applicability depends on jurisdiction/adoption/occupancy/exemption → code consultant or building control body; `prof-review` = judgement under competing objectives, or a life-safety inference drawn from a proxy → licensed professional. Closing block of the deliverable lists unresolved items grouped by class with counts, so the reviewer sees their workload before opening the plan.
- Exceptions / failure mode: Items answerable only by changing the engine go to `engine` (a product gap), not into any of the four — no review can close them. Failure symptom: 15 professional-review flags, 11 of which were one PDF away.

### UN-17 Ship an intended-use and out-of-scope statement with every design
- Rule: Every deliverable opens with what the model supports (layout, adjacency, circulation topology, access control, egress geometry as proxied) and what it must not be used for (cost, energy, structural adequacy, fire engineering, accessibility certification, occupancy loading, acoustic or daylight performance, anything time-based). Silence here is the worst available failure.
- Evidence: Writing permitted use down is the purpose of the digital-data protocol forms (AIA G201 sets "permitted uses"; G202 defines model LODs) and of the model-reporting literature, whose cards carry sections for "intended use… and out-of-scope uses" to prevent "misplaced claims that decrease the public's confidence in ML products". An LOD level is precisely a per-element reliability claim.
- Source: https://help.aiacontracts.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500010380681-faqs-g201-2013-project-digital-data-protocol-form-and-g202-2013-project-building-information-modeling-form — T3; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8980932/ — T2 (Garbin & Marques 2022 on Mitchell et al., *Model Cards for Model Reporting*, arXiv:1810.03993, FAT* 2019); https://bimforum.org/resource/lod-level-of-development-lod-specification/ — T3.
- Class: STANDARD
- Scope: universal
- Confidence: High
- Grid translation: Fixed 6-line header per deliverable: `USE`, `NOT FOR`, `SCALE`, `RESOLUTION`, `ASSUMPTIONS (n)`, `ESCALATIONS (by class, n)`. Validation: no metric may appear under `USE` unless computable from `walkable|blocked|door` + tags; `NOT FOR` is generated from the NR ledger (UN-05), never hand-typed.
- Exceptions / failure mode: A quick in-repo room layout may collapse the header to `NOT FOR` + assumption count, never to zero. Failure symptom: a screenshot reviewed as though it were a construction document.

### UN-18 Charge the model's own resolution error before criticising the design
- Rule: Compare every performance statement against the grid's discretisation error first; differences below the quantum are not findings. Publish the quantum once, then apply the threshold rule everywhere.
- Evidence: Verification checks "if the computational model fits the mathematical description" before validation may claim "if the model accurately represents the real world application" (ASME V&V). NIST's instruction to state uncertainty components "not taken into account" applies directly: quantisation is an unmodelled component unless disclosed.
- Source: https://www.asme.org/codes-standards/publications-information/verification-validation-uncertainty — T1/T3; NIST TN 1297 App. D6 — T1 (URL under UN-03).
- Class: ENGINEERING CONSTRAINT
- Scope: universal on this host
- Confidence: High (arithmetic from the grid spec)
- Grid translation: Emit once: length ±0.5 m at any boundary; area ±0.25 m² per tile, ±(W+H)·0.25 m² for a rectangular footprint; element position ±0.25 m (tile-centre convention); door width quantised to multiples of 1.0 m; circulation share ±1–2 points on a 60-room floor; wall thickness fixed at 0.5 m so real partition variation is unrepresentable; octile cost is exact in-model yet up to ~1.41× cheaper across an open hall than along a single-file corridor, so step-count gaps under ~10 % are not evidence of a better route. Report sub-quantum deltas as `indistinguishable at grid resolution`.
- Exceptions / failure mode: Counts (rooms, doors, portals, tag uses) are integers and exempt. Failure symptom: "Option B saves 0.3 m of travel" where one tile is 0.5 m.

### UN-19 Ask only for information that would change a decision; otherwise assume and flag
- Rule: Before asking the user anything, apply the value-of-information test: would a different answer change geometry, capacity or the compliance route? If not, do not ask. If yes and the answer is unavailable and is neither a secret nor an irreversible action, assume it, register it, continue.
- Evidence: Analytic standards require uncertainty to be expressed and explained rather than removed by stalling (ICD 203, UN-01); the project's operating rule makes reversible in-repo choices the agent's to decide and log, reserving stops for secrets, irreversible actions and genuinely missing information; deep-uncertainty practice rejects waiting for a single best estimate (Lempert et al. 2024).
- Source: as UN-01, UN-06; `AGENTS.md` "Decide, don't stall" — in-repo operating rule.
- Class: DESIGN PRINCIPLE
- Scope: universal
- Confidence: High
- Grid translation: Two-question gate before any user-facing question: `DECISION-CRITICAL?` (which tile group changes) and `REVERSIBLE WITHOUT ANSWER?` (can a proxy sit at `floor`-cheap cost). Not critical → assume + register row. Critical and unavailable → carry both branches only if the branch costs ≤5 % of the floor plate; otherwise BLOCKER. Validation: ≥2 unanswered tile-level questions in a report is a violation.
- Exceptions / failure mode: Missing *jurisdiction* and *occupancy classification* are legitimately decision-critical — they change code applicability, not decoration. Failure symptom: asking "what wall finish?" — or silently assuming US IBC for a project stated to be in England.

### UN-20 Say "I don't know" in the output; abstain or narrow rather than emit a Low number
- Rule: Where no defensible value exists, the deliverable is the narrower claim or an explicit abstention, phrased as a question with the missing input named. A Low-confidence number is permitted only together with a conservative default and a stated direction of error.
- Evidence: The abstention problem is measured (Kadavath et al. on self-knowledge; Zhou et al. ACL 2024: LMs "tend to be overconfident", users "rely heavily on LM generations"); silence is itself a decision — "you cannot not manage risk — even ignoring risk equates to a decision to accept it" (Oehmen & Kwakkel); and the analyst duty is to separate what is known from what is assessed (ICD 203).
- Source: as UN-01, UN-02, UN-14 — T1 / T2.
- Class: STANDARD
- Scope: universal
- Confidence: High
- Grid translation: Three abstention templates: (a) `NOT DETERMINED: <quantity> — requires <missing input>. Nearest defensible statement: <bound or relation>.`; (b) `NOT REPRESENTABLE: <property> — no field in the host model; ledger row <n>`; (c) `OUT OF SCOPE: <claim> — this deliverable is <USE>, not <NOT FOR>`. Direction-of-error rule: `designed to 3 tiles; if the true minimum is 44 in the design is over-wide, never under-wide, at the assumed scale`.
- Exceptions / failure mode: Abstention is not an escape hatch for work the agent can do — if a T1 artefact is one retrieval away, UN-16 class 5 applies instead. Failure symptom: a precise-looking figure that is a guess wearing a decimal point, or a report that abstains twelve times on things it could have looked up.

## Status taxonomy

Nine classes: the spec's eight plus this project's `Not representable in the host model` (UN-05). Tag
syntax is UN-01's. Each row: definition · entry criteria · what upgrades it · what downgrades it · tag.

| Class | Definition | Entry criteria (all must hold) | Upgrades when | Downgrades when | Tag |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Known** | Verified fact or grid arithmetic, re-checkable from the named artefact | Source opened at T1/T2/T3-primary, **or** derived arithmetically from the grid spec; codes name jurisdiction+edition | — (terminal for a fact) | Source found un-opened, mis-tiered, or superseded → Assumed/Estimated + correction line | `[U:known\|s:<token>\|c:High]` |
| **Assumed** | A choice made in place of missing information that the design relies on | Information genuinely absent; a different answer would change something; row exists in the register (UN-07) | Owner confirms, or research resolves it → Known | It breaks → convert to a risk/issue row and re-run affected checks | `[U:assumed\|c:<rung>\|owner:<role>]` |
| **Estimated** | Computed or rule-of-thumb value with a real spread that no better data makes exact *at this stage* | Band published with it (UN-04); digits truncated to the band (UN-03) | Higher definition stage → narrower band; received data → Known | Definition regresses → widen the band | `[U:est\|band:±x%\|stage:S2]` |
| **Unknown** | The model *could* carry the answer and one exists; the agent lacks it | Nothing retrieved answers it; it is not a capability gap (else NR) | Research or a brief answer → Known/Assumed | Found to be unencodable → re-tag NR | `[U:unknown\|q:<question>\|owner:<role>]` |
| **Requires research** | Unknown **and** the closure route is documentary | The agent can name the next artefact to open | Done → Known | Answer turns out to need licensed judgement → classes 6-8 | `[U:research\|next:<artefact>]` |
| **Requires engineering verification** | Needs analysis whose inputs the model lacks (loads, spans, deflection, sizing) | A competent engineer would not accept the geometry alone as an answer | Certified by the discipline → Known | Turns out regulatory → code verification | `[U:eng-verify\|disc:<struct\|mep\|fire>]` |
| **Requires code verification** | Clause applies but value/applicability depends on jurisdiction, adoption, occupancy class, exemptions | The doubt is about the clause's applicability, not about arithmetic | Adopted local text confirmed (clause quoted) → Known | Needs sign-off → professional review | `[U:code-verify\|clause:<n>\|juris:<UNKNOWN>]` |
| **Requires professional review** | Judgement under competing objectives, or a life-safety inference drawn from a proxy | UN-15/UN-16 conditions hold; risk is not arithmetic | Reviewed and accepted → Assumed with reviewer named | Proven documentary → research | `[U:prof-review\|role:<who>]` |
| **Not representable in the host model** | No field (tile states, tags, portals, flood-fill rooms, octile cost) can encode the property | Verified against the schema and the capability ledger; proxy option already evaluated | **Never to Known.** Only: attach a proxy (then the *proxy* is known, the property stays NR) or change the engine | Proxy removed, or a new field appears → reclassify (usually to Known) | `[U:NR\|needs:<missing field>]` |

**NR vs Unknown.** They differ in *who can close the gap and by what act*. `Unknown` is a knowledge gap
inside an adequate model: someone reads something and the tag changes. `NR` is a capability gap in the
model itself: no reading, asking or computing produces the value, because the representation cannot hold
it. Their failure modes differ too — an `Unknown` handled by asking is only slow, whereas an `NR` handled
by "research" is wasted effort and an `NR` handled by encoding a plausible tile value is falsification
(it manufactures a fake fire rating, height or structural bay). Corollary: NR items appear in the
`NOT FOR` header (UN-17), never inside the numbers table. Test for which one you have: if more data
could reduce it, it is not NR (Der Kiureghian & Ditlevsen 2009, UN-02).

## Assumption register schema

Five mandatory fields (deliverable spec §38) plus three governance fields (UN-08):

```
A<n>  Assumption           <one clause, no hedging>
      Reason               <gap in brief / no source / no model field / decision>
      Confidence           High | Medium | Low + basis        (UN-10: rung + evidence, never a %)
      Impact if wrong      <what changes in tiles, capacity or compliance, and its direction>
      Verification needed  research | eng-verify | code-verify | prof-review | none   (UN-16)
      Owner                brief-owner | engine | agent | code-consultant | structural | mep | fire
      Revisit trigger      <event or artefact arrival forcing re-check>
      Reversibility        tile-local | floor | section | concept      (UN-09)
```

Extras are justified by function, not taste: a false assumption must be able to surface as a tracked
risk/issue, which requires an owner and a review cadence (PRINCE2 RAID, T4; ISO 31000's monitor-and-
review step, T1 by title); and the reversibility grade is what decides stop-and-ask versus
proceed-and-flag (UN-09; one-way/two-way doors, T3).

Worked register — tile-grid hotel floor (pre-release standing order ⇒ several rows are "assume, don't ask"):

| ID | Assumption | Reason | Confidence | Impact if wrong | Verification needed | Owner | Revisit trigger | Reversibility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | 1 tile = 0.5 m | Brief convention; engine stores no physical unit (grep found none) | High as convention, **Low as a claim about the rendered world** | Every metre statement scales identically; a 3-tile corridor stops being 1.5 m and stops clearing 44 in (1.12 m) below a 0.38 m/tile scale | none (product decision; record in glossary) | brief-owner | any tile-size or render-scale change | concept |
| A2 | One blocked tile *is* the wall | No thickness or material field | High in-model; the real partition (0.10–0.20 m) it stands for is unrepresented | Room interiors are systematically smaller relative to practice; net-to-gross ratios are not comparable to real hotel data | research (only if such a comparison is reported) | agent | an asset gains a thickness field | concept |

| A4 | Guest room = 2 persons unless tagged single | Occupancy is a design input the brief did not give | Medium, band ±0.4 persons/room | Room count drives laundry, back-of-house, egress width and lift demand | research (adopted code + brief) | brief-owner | first occupancy model in the sim | floor |

wipe-at-any-time project.

## Confidence calibration

**Rung + basis, always (UN-10).** Rungs are defined by evidence properties, not by mood: **High** = T1/T2
primary opened, or grid arithmetic, or ≥2 independent T2/T3 agreeing; **Medium** = one T3, or a T1 body not
opened (record/title only), or a T4 mirror of a paywalled primary; **Low** = T4/T5 only, a recollected
rule, or a proxy for something the model cannot hold. Two anti-patterns are refused: *numeric confidence*
from a qualitative judgement ("85%"), and *decimal-precision dimensions from a rule of thumb*. The second
is the spec's named lie: a rule of thumb carries ±20–40 % by construction (AACE: the tightest class is
−10/+15 %, early classes −50/+100 %), so `12.7 m²` asserts 0.8 % resolution from a method 400× wider.
Correct: `≈13 m² (band ±25 %, stage S1)`. The grid adds its own quantum on top — a rectangular footprint
moves ±(W+H)·0.25 m² per tile (UN-03, UN-18) — so even the integer m² is not the precision it appears.

**Aggregating through a chain of derived numbers (UN-11).** Hotel chain, tagged:
`rooms 60 [known|derived]` → `net/room 18 m² ±25% [est|T4 heuristic|Low]` → `gross +wall tax +25% circ
[est|chain of two ests|Low]` → `occupant load [Medium: factor T1 opened × area Low]` → `required egress
width [Medium]` → `corridor 3 tiles [known|derived, exact-in-model]` → `"therefore compliant"` REFUSED,
weakest link is Low. Independent corroborating sources may lift a rung; a chain never lifts itself; a High
on a relation ("3 tiles is wider than 2") is exact-in-model only. When an input's status changes, every
dependent line is re-tagged — that is the mechanical content of a `Revisit trigger`.

**Where calibration is weakest here.** The same number is Known for a topology claim and Low for a
performance claim ("this room has one door" vs "guests will choose this corridor"). Use one tag set for
both and let `NOT FOR` (UN-17) carry the difference, rather than inflating a rung.

## Provenance rules

**What counts as a citation.** An artefact actually opened this session (URL fetched, PDF text extracted,
code file read), given as body/author + title + edition + clause or page + URL + tier (UN-12, UN-14);
repo facts cite `file:line`. A remembered book is a method-level citation, marked `no URL retrieved`, and
can never carry a number. Codes additionally require `jurisdiction:edition:clause`, with "model code,
verify local adoption" where adoption is unknown, and occupant/width factors paired with the occupancy
classification and exemption condition they came from (per `building-codes.md`).

**Structure of a record.** Keep PROV-O's triad so provenance is checkable rather than decorative: the
*value* (Entity), the *derivation* (Activity — `flood-fill`, `A*`, `tiles × 0.25 m²`, `clause → tiles`),
and the responsible *source or actor* (Agent — opened document, user brief, engine constant, or the
agent's own assumption), with `wasDerivedFrom` / `hadPrimarySource` chains explicit. Recording the
document but not the derivation is why a wrong factor survives next to a correct citation.

**Flag phrasing.** Reuse the house block unchanged, plus one line so the flag self-routes:

```
ISSUE / CHECK / ATTEMPTED SOURCE / VERIFICATION: verified(T1)|partial(T3)|UNVERIFIED
/ REQUIRED DATA / CONSERVATIVE DEFAULT / FLAG-LEVEL: BLOCKER|REVIEW|ADVISORY
/ CLASS: unknown|research|eng-verify|code-verify|prof-review|NR
```

Name the missing input, not your unease ("adopted local edition unknown" beats "needs checking"); always
pair with the conservative default actually designed to; prefer `BLOCKER` where the item removes a way out.

**Ban-list.** (1) No compliance or approval claims (UN-15). (2) No invented dimensions, areas, distances,
spacings, occupancies or clause numbers (UN-14, UN-20). (3) No laundering T4/T5 into rules, and no
re-quoting one source to fake agreement (UN-13, UN-11). (4) No confidence percentage the source does not
state (UN-10). (5) No digits the weakest link cannot support (UN-03). (6) Never encode an unrepresentable
property as geometry, and never treat unconsumed tag text as data (UN-05). (7) Never report model-exact
counts or octile steps as real-world measurements (UN-18). (8) Never drop the register or the `NOT FOR`
line because the deliverable is small (UN-07, UN-17). (9) Never let a stale status stand: a tag predating
an engine change or an earlier revision is a false tag (UN-08).

## Weak or contested

- **The scale assumption is the weakest load-bearing claim in the skill.** `1 tile = 0.5 m` is a brief
  convention with no engine corroboration (verified negative), so every metre statement inherits A1 at
  `c:Low`. Nothing here demonstrates that the rendered geometry is 0.5 m per tile.
- **Kent's numeric equivalents are mirror-sourced and possibly not authoritative.** The primary PDF
  resisted extraction, so certain 100 / almost certain 93 / probable 75 / about even 50 / probably not 30
  / almost certainly not 7 / impossible 0 come from a tertiary summary; later US practice separates
  *confidence* from *likelihood* instead of pinning percentages to words, which is the position taken
  here. Do not present the numbers as a standard.
- **The epistemic/aleatory boundary is model-relative.** The cited reliability paper itself asks whether
  the distinction matters, and the ML literature treats the split as a reparameterisation choice. Use it
  operationally (do the work vs publish a band), never metaphysically.
- **Borrowed ladders are analogies, and their numbers are second-hand.** GRADE arbitrates clinical
  evidence and the IPCC note modelled climate claims: this file imports their *structure* (rung + basis,
  named downgrade domains, confidence ≠ likelihood), never their thresholds. The AACE bands come from a
  vendor explainer of a paywalled RP that itself calls such ranges "guidelines, not targets" — and they
  belong to cost estimating, which this project has no model for, so only the band-publishing discipline
  is taken (UN-04).
- **Citation-integrity prevalence is a young finding, and the human-side effect is unproven here.**
  GhostCite/HALLMARK are recent, unreplicated preprints and HALLMARK's own premise is that detector
  quality cannot yet be measured — treat 1.07 % and the 53-paper incident as motivation, not as a base
  rate. Model-side overconfidence is documented; the automation-bias evidence on *reviewers* (AI & Society
  2025, Microsoft's overreliance review, the CHI 2024 reliance study) was located but not read, so
  UN-01/UN-20's rationale is stated as direction — labels must be visible and actionable — with no
  effect-size claim.
- **Ordinal risk matrices deliberately excluded.** 5×5 heat-map scoring is ubiquitous, but the critiques
  of it outrun its defences, and a computed score is exactly the false precision this file bans: impact
  and confidence stay named grades with written bases (UN-10).
- **Jurisdictional thinness of UN-15/UN-16.** The "who may certify" evidence is English (GOV.UK, BSR);
  the US equivalents (state boards, NCARB model law, third-party inspection) could not be read this pass,
  so the rule is stated at principle level with its evidence jurisdiction named. No building-type
  dimensional standard appears anywhere in this file, and no hospitality-specific assumption practice was
  found — the worked register is derived from this brief and the grid, not from hospitality literature.
