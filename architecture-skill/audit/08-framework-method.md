### 1.7 Planarity & rectangular duals — MATHEMATICS (AG-03, AG-04, AG-05, AG-14, AG-17)
- **CLAIM AG-03**: "Region adjacency graphs of plane maps are planar"; Kuratowski criterion for the obstruction; "Contact graphs of isothetic rectangles are planar and 'play an important role in … architecture (floor planning)'" (Buchsbaum et al.); Koebe "any planar graph (and only a planar graph) can be expressed as the contact graph of disks"; linear-time Kuratowski extraction (Williamson, *J. ACM* 1984). Grid rule `|E| > 3n−6 ⇒ non-planar (n ≥ 3)`.
- **CLAIM AG-04**: "A group of rooms that must all touch each other is capped by planarity … **Clique ≥ 5 is impossible on any 2-D grid** … **Clique = 4 is only achievable if at least one member is non-rectangular (L/U-shaped)** or if the four meet in a degenerate point junction, so with a rectangular-room kit-of-parts it is a warning, not a plan"; quotes Bekos et al.: "Every graph that can be represented this way [axis-aligned rectangles, non-zero-length boundary contacts] is planar and every triangle in such a graph is a facial triangle. **These two conditions are also sufficient** to guarantee a rectangle representation"; Koźmiński & Kinnen's 4-triangulation characterisation; "paths are the only trees that have such duals".
- **WHAT THE SOURCES ACTUALLY SAYS**: fetched `arxiv.org/abs/1403.4861` (record) and the HTML v2 text. **The first quoted sentence is verbatim**: "Every graph that can be represented this way is planar and every triangle in such a graph is a facial triangle." **The sufficiency sentence was not located in the retrieved text**, and the abstract page carries none of the rectangular-dual/Koźmiński material. The Buchsbaum PDF was not fetched (budget).
- **MATHEMATICAL VERDICT (checked directly, not by citation)**:
  - `3n − 6` simple-planar bound, `2n − 4` triangle-free/bipartite bound (AG-17), and `μ_max = e − n + 1 ≤ (3n−6) − n + 1 = 2n − 5` for ringness (AG-14) — **all correct** for connected simple planar graphs; AG-03's own note that multi-doors are not extra adjacency edges is the right guard.
  - "Region adjacency graphs are planar" — correct **given** non-degenerate regions (Ungar-style conditions, which AG-19 enforces) — and AG-03's exception list (isolated pillar tile, 8-connected diagonal wall, windmill junction) is exactly the failure set. Well-asked, well-answered.
  - "Clique ≥ 5 impossible" — **correct** (Kuratowski; K5 non-planar).
  - "Clique = 4 impossible with four rectangles" — **correct, and provable more strongly than the file dares.** Elementary argument (mine, not the papers'): in any gap-free rectangular dual of ≥ 2 rectangles, the first guillotine-style division of the outer rectangle puts one region on one side of a full cut; with 4 rectangles the maximum number of distinct adjacency pairs realisable is **5** (case split: first cut separates 1|3 — the singleton touches at most 3, the remaining ≤ 2 pairs are collinear-chain adjacent; first cut separates 2|2 — ≤ 2 internal + ≤ 2 cross pairs). K4 needs 6. Therefore **K4 has no rectangular dual**, and T-junction/degenerate-point geometry cannot rescue it, since a point contact is not an edge contact under the file's own collinear-wall rule. The literature agrees in shape: rectangular duals require a 4-triangulation-shaped graph, and K4 has no degree-4 vertex to delete.
  - **The genuine subtlety the file half-sees**: the *sufficiency* quote ("planar + all triangles facial ⇒ rectangle representation") cannot be applied naively, because K4 satisfies both conditions under the embedding with a triangular outer face — yet has no rectangular dual. The resolution is that the quoted sufficiency in that literature covers **contact layouts that allow gaps/holes** (and carries extra conditions about which triangles are filled and which face is outer), *not* the gap-free dual case. So the two sentences AG-04 quotes are about **two different problems** (contact-with-gaps vs dual-gap-free), and the file's Confidence line flags precisely that ambiguity.
- **PROBLEM**: mild *under*-claim plus one unverified quotation: AG-04 leaves the K4 case "an open check, not a theorem" and its Weak bullet asks for a future pass to tighten it — the elementary count above closes it. The sufficiency sentence is quoted without having been retrieved.
- **EVIDENCE STATUS: AG-03 SUPPORTED (math + verbatim quote). AG-04 "≥5 impossible" SUPPORTED; "K4 needs a non-rectangular member" SUPPORTED-by-proof (present in-file as Medium-confidence); the sufficiency quote PARTIALLY SUPPORTED (first half verbatim, second half unretrieved); Koźmiński & Kinnen and "paths are the only trees" UNVERIFIED (records exist; text not fetched). AG-05's frontier bound: SUPPORTED (it is the file's own arithmetic and it is correct).**
- **ACTION: KEEP, and tighten AG-04**: state the slicing/count argument as the reason a 4-clique is unrealisable with rectangular footprints, promote that clause from Medium to High, and separate the two literature families explicitly ("rectangle *contact* graphs, gaps allowed" vs "rectangular *duals*, gap-free") so the quoted sufficiency is never read across them. Mark the "also sufficient" sentence `quote not re-verified this pass` until Buchsbaum §1 is opened.
- *Closes fully*: fetch Buchsbaum et al. (arXiv cs/0611107) §1–2 text for the sufficiency statement and the "paths are the only trees" sentence.
