# floor-g lobby - tile-exact plan, revision 3. Every coordinate is a TILE (0.5 m).
# Walls are 1 tile thick, placed one run at a time; doors one tile at a time; fixtures one at a time.
# Canvas 116 x 76. Street ring 8 -> facade ring at x=8/107, y=8/67. Plate interior x 9..106, y 9..66.
#
# Revision 2 scored 5/10: the north half read as architecture and the south half read as a field.
# Revision 3 gives the south half its own rooms:
#   - the brasserie takes the whole south-west as a TERRACE, joined to the dining room by a 9-tile
#     arch, so the restaurant is an L and the west of the plate is programmed floor to facade
#   - the street entry moves east off the restaurant's flank to cols 25..28, where the arrival axis
#     runs north between two pinch screens into the forecourt - shorter route, real procession
#   - the desk run stays off that axis under its screen, with the walled duty office behind it
#   - the planted water court stays as the centre of gravity of the great room
# Walls share lines rather than doubling: the seed guard "no wall is two tiles thick" is enforced
# by `npm run test:unit`. GT-27: fixtures stay >= 3 tiles from a wall whose far side is another space.
